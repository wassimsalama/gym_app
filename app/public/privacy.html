Privacy

Last updated 7 September 2026

Gym App is a personal project run by one person. It records what you log about your own training and nothing else. There is no advertising, no tracking, no analytics SDK, and your data is never sold or shared.

The short version. Everything stored is something you typed into the app. Nobody else can see it. You can delete all of it from inside the app at any time, and deletion is immediate and permanent.

What is stored

DataWhy
Email address and passwordTo sign you in. Handled by Supabase Auth; the password is never visible to this app.
Body weight, by dateTo draw your trend and estimate maintenance calories.
Calories, protein, carbs, fatTo show intake against maintenance.
Workouts: exercises, sets, weights, reps, notesTo pre-fill your next session and detect personal records.
Goals: start weight, target weight, datesTo show progress and project a finish date.
Progress photosOnly if you add them. Stored privately; see below.
Display unit (kg or lb)To show weights the way you prefer.
Which days you opened the appA date per day, so the developer can see how many people use it. Nothing about what you did.

What is not stored

  • No IP addresses or approximate location.
  • No device fingerprints, browser or user-agent records.
  • No advertising or analytics SDKs of any kind.
  • No cookies. See storage below.
  • No record of individual pages you visit or buttons you press.

Health data

Body weight, nutrition and training records are health-related data, which most privacy law treats as sensitive and requiring your clear agreement. Yours is given by choosing to enter it. You are never required to log anything, every field is optional, and you can stop and delete it all at any time.

Photos

Progress photos are stored in a private bucket that is not readable from the open internet. When you view one, the app generates a signed link that works only for your session and expires within the hour. Photos are resized on your device before upload. They are never used for anything but showing them back to you.

Storage on your device

The app sets no cookies. It uses two browser storage mechanisms, both strictly necessary for it to function:

  • localStorage — holds your sign-in session so you are not asked to log in on every page, and caches the last screen you loaded so it still shows something with no connection.
  • IndexedDB — holds anything you log while offline until it can be sent. Without it, a workout logged in a basement gym would be lost.

Neither is used to track you, and neither leaves your device except as the data described above. Signing out clears both.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Delete account in the app. Your weight history, workouts, nutrition logs, goals and photos are removed immediately and permanently. There is no soft delete, no grace period and no archived copy. Ask by email if you would rather it were done for you.

Your rights

You may ask for access to your data, correction of it, deletion, or a copy in a portable form, and you may withdraw your agreement at any time. In practice the app already shows you everything it holds and lets you delete all of it yourself, immediately. For anything else, email the address below.

Users are mainly in Canada, so Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) applies; if you are elsewhere, your local law may give you further rights and those are honoured too. If you are unhappy with how a request was handled, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or to the equivalent authority where you live.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry, because a training history is only useful over years. If the project shuts down, accounts will be deleted and users told beforehand.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or who sees it, the app will say so before the change applies. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
